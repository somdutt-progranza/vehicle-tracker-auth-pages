Delete your KarvIQ account

You can permanently delete your account and everything attached to it directly from the app. Here's exactly what that does.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the KarvIQ app and sign in with the account you want to remove.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Deletion happens immediately and can't be undone.

No waiting period, no support ticket — the request is processed the moment you confirm.

What happens to your data

Deleted permanently

  • Profile — name, email, phone, avatar
  • Every vehicle and its service history
  • Warranties & maintenance schedules
  • Uploaded bills and documents
  • Notification history

Retained separately

  • Subscription billing records, held by Stripe for tax and fraud-prevention compliance
  • Not visible or usable by KarvIQ after deletion

Can't sign in?

If you no longer have access to the app, email support@progranza.com from the address on your account and we'll delete it on your behalf.
